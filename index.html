Ningsen Wang (王宁森)

I’m a senior in Computer Science at Fudan University focused on medical AI, especially multimodal vision-language models. I currently build data-centric pipelines for high-quality medical VQA, explore RL-style fine-tuning of large VLMs for trustworthy step-by-step reasoning and medical deep research agent. I am currently a research intern in the UCSC VLAA (remote), working with Prof. Yuyin Zhou and and her PhD student Xiaoke Huang . My goal is reproducible, open, and clinically useful AI.
