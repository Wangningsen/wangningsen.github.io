I’m a senior in Computer Science at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/) focused on medical AI, especially multimodal vision-language models. I currently build data-centric pipelines for high-quality medical VQA, explore RL-style fine-tuning of large VLMs for trustworthy step-by-step reasoning and medical deep research agent. I am currently a research intern in the [UCSC VLAA](https://ucsc-vlaa.github.io/index.html) (remote), working with Prof. [Yuyin Zhou](https://yuyinzhou.github.io/) and her PhD student [Xiaoke Huang](https://xk-huang.github.io/). My goal is reproducible, open, and clinically useful AI.
